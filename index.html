Why Switched Games?

Most games require two hands, complex controllers, and fast reflexes. We build games that only need one or two inputs — spacebar, enter key, mouse click, or screen touch — and we host a free arcade to prove it.

Our mission

Games that need one or two inputs. Nothing more.

SwitchedGames is dedicated to creating, collecting, and promoting browser-based games that are fully playable with a single switch or two. That means the spacebar, the enter key, a mouse click, a screen touch, or any external adaptive switch.

Auto-scan mode is built into every game — no setup, no downloads, no barriers. Most players only need one input. Independence is the goal.

We believe fun, engaging, and genuinely challenging games should be accessible to everyone — especially people who only have access to one or two switches. These games are built for independent play, not just participation.

1switch needed to play most games
2max inputs for any game
🆓always free, forever
🌐any browser, any device

Free arcade

Play at Benny's Hub

Our free and open-source arcade — sports, puzzles, word games, and more. Every game is built from the ground up for one and two switch users. Spacebar, enter key, click, or touch — no account, no install, no barriers. Just press and play.

The entire hub — menus, game selection, settings, everything — is navigatable with just one or two switches by design. A user who can only access one or two buttons can independently browse and play the full arcade on their own.

Free switch kits

Apply to Get a Free 2-Button Switch Kit

NARBE Foundation raises funds to donate switch kits to individuals and families who need them. Each kit is everything you need to start playing immediately — no tech knowledge required.

  • 🔘
    2 Accessibility SwitchesLarge, easy-to-press buttons with 3.5mm connectors
  • 🔌
    USB AdapterConverts switch presses into keyboard signals
  • 🔀
    3.5mm Y-Splitter CableConnects both switches to the adapter at once
  • 📎
    Adhesive Velcro StripsMount your switches wherever is most comfortable

🎯 Default Input Mapping

Switch 1 → SPACE Spacebar
Switch 2 → ↵ Enter Key

🎮 Apply for Your Free Kit

Kits are distributed quarterly on a first-come, first-served basis. United States only. No cost — just fill out the application.

✅ Submit an Application

Full setup guide at narbefoundation.org/switchkit

⚠️ Recreational use only. These kits are for gaming and computer interaction — not medical or AAC purposes. For AAC hardware, please consult a Speech-Language Pathologist or Occupational Therapist.
