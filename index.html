What is a switch?

Not Nintendo — a different kind of switch.

A switch is a single button — physical or on-screen — that someone uses as their only way to interact with a computer. For many people with physical disabilities, it's the only input they have access to. A large press button mounted to a wheelchair, a sip-and-puff device, or just the spacebar — all of these are switches. SwitchedGames is built so anyone using one can play real, engaging games fully and independently.

Free switch kits

Apply to Get a Free 2-Button Switch Kit

NARBE Foundation raises funds to donate switch kits to individuals and families who need them. Each kit is everything you need to start playing immediately — no tech knowledge required.

  • 🔘
    2 Accessibility SwitchesLarge, easy-to-press buttons with 3.5mm connectors
  • 🔌
    USB AdapterConverts switch presses into keyboard signals
  • 🔀
    3.5mm Y-Splitter CableConnects both switches to the adapter at once
  • 📎
    Adhesive Velcro StripsMount your switches wherever is most comfortable

🎯 Default Input Mapping

Switch 1 → SPACE Spacebar
Switch 2 → ↵ Enter Key

🎮 Apply for Your Free Kit

Kits are distributed quarterly on a first-come, first-served basis. United States only. No cost — just fill out the application.

✅ Submit an Application

Full setup guide at narbefoundation.org/switchkit

⚠️ Recreational use only. These kits are for gaming and computer interaction — not medical or AAC purposes. For AAC hardware, please consult a Speech-Language Pathologist or Occupational Therapist.
