What is a switch?

Not Nintendo — a different kind of switch.

A switch is a single button — physical or on-screen — that someone uses as their only way to interact with a computer. For many people with physical disabilities, it's the only input they have access to. A large press button mounted to a wheelchair, a sip-and-puff device, or just the spacebar — all of these are switches. SwitchedGames is built so anyone using one can play real, engaging games fully and independently.

Benny’s Hub is the flagship project NARBE LLC develops for Ben, releasing games through the hub and SwitchedGames alongside NARBE Foundation. The hub’s menus, settings, and games are navigable with one or two switches, so players can browse and play independently.

Adaptive Switch Kit Program

Apply to Get a Free 2-Button Switch Kit

Through our Adaptive Switch Kit Program, NARBE Foundation provides free 2-button switch kits to individuals and families who need them. The program is funded entirely by our community and partners. Each kit is everything you need to start playing immediately — no tech knowledge required.

  • 2 Accessibility SwitchesLarge, easy-to-press buttons with 3.5mm connectors
  • USB AdapterConverts switch presses into keyboard signals
  • 3.5mm Y-Splitter CableConnects both switches to the adapter at once
  • Adhesive Velcro StripsMount your switches wherever is most comfortable

Default Input Mapping

Switch 1 → SPACE Spacebar
Switch 2 → ↵ Enter Key

Apply for Your Free Kit

Kits are offered through an application process, subject to availability. Selection details are provided in the application. United States only, at no cost to you. To apply, please sign up for the NARBE Foundation newsletter. The application link will be included when the newsletter goes out.
