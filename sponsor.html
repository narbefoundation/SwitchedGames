Our story

We're NARBE. This is our family.

We're Ari, Nancy, and Ben. Ben is Ari's little brother who has a condition called H-ABC — a rare, progressive neurological disorder. We are full-time, live-in caregivers for Ben, and Ben is the reason everything here exists.

Ben's condition is progressive. Over time, it took away his voice and his mobility. He spent nearly a decade with no meaningful access to technology — no games, no independent play, nothing designed for how he communicates or moves. In 2022, Ben moved in with Ari and Nancy, and that's when everything changed.

We started building Benny's Hub — a free, browser-based arcade designed specifically for Ben. Every game works with one or two switch inputs: the spacebar, the enter key, a mouse click, a screen touch, or any external adaptive switch. We built it around what Ben actually needed, what he responded to, what made him light up. It changed things for him. Games built just for him, where he could play independently, on his own terms.

The moment we saw what it did for Ben, we couldn't stop asking: why aren't there more games like this? Why doesn't a platform like this already exist?

We listen to Ben first and foremost — he tells us exactly what he wants and needs, and we build around that. We also listen to occupational therapists, speech-language pathologists, families, and caregivers to understand what's actually needed beyond our own household.

Ben isn't just the inspiration — he's a collaborator. He helps with game ideas, mechanics, and is our primary tester for everything we ship. He plays these games every single day. We watch what he gravitates toward, what keeps his attention, what makes him laugh, and what he comes back to. That feedback loop is built directly into how we build. And we're noticing something: more and more people are joining in. Families, caregivers, classmates — people who don't use switches at all — are playing alongside switch users and having a blast.

And anyone can play. Many of our games are collaborative and multiplayer, designed with multiple input methods — so families, classmates, and caregivers can play alongside switch users at the same time.

To make this sustainable, we founded NARBE Foundation — a 501(c)(3) nonprofit — to keep the games free and run a switch kit donation program, pairing hardware with families who need it.

NARBE LLC — that's us, to keep things official, and the development team — is what actually builds the games. We fund that work ourselves and now through sponsorships. We want to keep building for Ben and for others, and this is how we can do it.
